Your Riftbound collection, decks and matches, in one app.

Runekeep is a companion app for Riftbound players on Android. Track the cards you own, build decks and keep score at the table.

All your data stays on your phone: no account needed.

Android Works offline No account Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

Collection

Record the cards you own for every set, see your completion, filter by domain or rarity, keep wishlists and browse your binder.

Deckbuilder

Build legend, champion, main deck, runes and battlefields with live validity checks, see what you are missing and export a tournament decklist.

Match scorekeeper

Replaces pen and paper at the table. You record points by hand; the app does not play the game for you.

Your own stats

A personal log of your games and the events you attend, kept only on your device.

Your data

Stays on your phone

Collection, decks and matches are saved only on your device. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded.

Back it up yourself

Export a backup file and keep it on Drive or wherever you like: uninstalling the app deletes its data.
